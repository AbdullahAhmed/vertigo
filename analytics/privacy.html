Anonymous gameplay statistics

We collect first-party gameplay counts to understand whether players can finish the tower: visits and plays, new games and continues, stair attempts and distance, falls, jumps, crumbling slabs, creature encounters, low-nerve time, roof arrivals, switch activations and completed collapses. We also record active play time and coarse performance totals, platform (mobile or desktop), game build, and session timestamps.

A random browser identifier in local storage estimates unique players. The server stores a keyed digest of it. We do not collect your name, email, precise location, browsing history, raw user agent or an identifying device fingerprint. The hosting server uses your connection IP to estimate your country and, when available, region. We store only that rough estimate, not your raw IP, city or coordinates. This uses no browser location permission and sends no IP to an external lookup service. VPNs and mobile networks can make the estimate inaccurate. Normal hosting access logs may contain IP addresses; the analytics store uses only short-lived rotating address digests for abuse prevention.

The dashboard is private. We may publish aggregated statistics later. Browser IDs and session records will not be part of public statistics. Metrics are retained for the game's analysis; rate-limit records expire after two days.

This choice does not affect saves or gameplay. It takes effect for game tabs after they reload. Clearing browser storage resets this preference and the anonymous identifier.
