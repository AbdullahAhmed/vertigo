# Figure face

`figure-face.png` is original AI-generated artwork created for VERTIGO with OpenAI image generation. No paid asset pack or third-party character artwork was used. The original PNG is embedded by `build.js`, decoded before the title, and tagged sRGB at runtime.

Art direction: an androgynous adult face emerging from black smoke; pallid charcoal skin, concrete and ash textures, asymmetric sunken eyes, sculpted facial planes and a stretched screaming mouth. No gore. Transparent smoke edges and restrained ivory highlights. The game uses the image as creature art, not as an image of the protagonist.

Prompt: “Create a production-ready original horror-game creature face texture, portrait 1024x1536, genuinely transparent background. Only a disembodied androgynous adult face, no body, clothing or hair. Intended as a very brief face emerging from black smoke in a low-poly first-person concrete-tower horror game. Front view, slight asymmetric tilt, prominent sculpted cheekbones and brow, deeply sunken asymmetrical eye cavities, eyes almost entirely black with only tiny wet reflections, jaw unnaturally stretched into a silent scream. Mouth a deep near-black cavity with just subtle irregular tooth fragments, no bright tooth row. Pallid charcoal-gray weathered skin like cracked concrete and ash, detailed fine creases, scraped textures, convincing shaded three-dimensional anatomy. Sparse desaturated ivory highlights from upper left; edges fade naturally into thin torn black smoke and transparency. Keep most of the face dark but distinct readable facial planes. Expression raw dread and anguish, uncanny and human, not a commercial Halloween mask, no cartoon or emoji, no Scream-movie mask, no skull, no gore, no bloody wounds. Single centered head with generous transparent margin, no frame, no words, no watermarks. This is a game texture asset, not a poster or scene.”
